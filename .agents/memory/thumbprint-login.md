---
name: Thumbprint login meaning
description: Product meaning of the fingerprint thumb artwork shown on the Login screen.
---

The fingerprint thumb artwork on the Login screen represents the “Login with thumbprint” action.

**Why:** The artwork is an intentional authentication affordance, not merely a visual security accent.

**How to apply:** Preserve that meaning in labels, accessibility text, and future interaction work. If actual device biometric authentication is added, connect the thumb control to that flow rather than treating it as a passive image.
